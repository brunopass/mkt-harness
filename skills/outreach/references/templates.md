# Outreach templates

Skeletons to adapt, not to paste. `{fact}` = a specific public fact from the lead's research. Rewrite natively per
language; the pt/es lines below are examples of natural phrasing, not translations to reuse verbatim.

## LinkedIn connection note (≤ 300 chars)
- en: "Hi {first}, saw {fact}. I work with {persona plural} on {problem} — would be good to connect."
- pt: "Oi {first}, vi {fact}. Trabalho com {persona} em {problema} — faz sentido a gente se conectar."
- es: "Hola {first}, vi {fact}. Trabajo con {persona} en {problema}, me encantaría conectar."
No pitch, no link.

## First DM after connecting / cold DM (40-90 words)
en:
> Thanks for connecting, {first}. {fact} caught my eye — {one-line why it matters: usually that brings {pain}}.
> We helped {similar company} {result with number} by {mechanism in 6-10 words}.
> Would a 2-minute summary of how be useful, or not a priority right now?

pt:
> Valeu por aceitar, {first}. Vi {fact} — normalmente é aí que {dor} aperta.
> Ajudamos a {empresa parecida} a {resultado} com {mecanismo}.
> Te mando um resumo de 2 minutos de como foi, ou não é prioridade agora?

es:
> Gracias por conectar, {first}. Vi {fact}; suele ser justo cuando {dolor} se complica.
> Ayudamos a {empresa similar} a {resultado} con {mecanismo}.
> ¿Te paso un resumen de 2 minutos o ahora no es prioridad?

## Step 2: value add (same thread, day 3-4)
> {first}, one thing that might help regardless: {2-line tip / link to our resource}. Most {persona} we talk to
> {common mistake}.

## Step 3: new angle (day 7-8)
- Objection-preempt: "A lot of {persona} tell us '{objection verbatim}'. Fair — here's how {customer} handled it: {one line}."
- Peer proof: "{Similar company} was in the same spot after {similar fact}. {Result}."

## Step 4: breakup (day 12-14)
- en: "Should I close the loop on this, {first}? If timing's off, no worries — I won't keep writing."
- pt: "Fecho por aqui, {first}? Se não for o momento, tudo bem — não vou insistir."
- es: "¿Lo dejamos aquí, {first}? Si no es el momento, sin problema; no insisto."

## Cold email (50-125 words)
Subject: `{fact-topic} + {problem}` (2-6 words), e.g. "nova unidade + agenda", "pregunta sobre reservas".
> Hi {first},
>
> {fact}. {One sentence connecting it to the pain, ideally with a number from our proof bank.}
>
> {Mechanism: what we do, in one sentence.} {Customer} got {result} in {time}.
>
> Worth a 10-minute look, or should I not bother?
>
> {Name}
> {Role}, {Company}
(opt-out line + sender address are appended automatically)

## Instagram DM (B2C/local business)
> Oi {first}! Vi {fact} no perfil de vocês 👏 Uma pergunta rápida: {pergunta genuína sobre a dor}?
> (If they answer, move to the inbox playbook. Don't send links in the first DM.)

## WhatsApp (opt-in or they wrote first only)
> Oi {first}, aqui é {name} da {brand}. Você pediu {what they asked for} — segue: {link}.
> Posso te fazer uma pergunta rápida pra ver se faz sentido pra você?
Keep it 1-3 short lines; no bulk-style formatting; no voice notes on first contact.

## Re-engagement (lead went quiet after replying once)
> {first}, last time you mentioned {their words}. Did that get solved? If not, {one new useful thing}.
