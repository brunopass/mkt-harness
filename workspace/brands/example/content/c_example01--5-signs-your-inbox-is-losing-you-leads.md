## Slides

### Your leads aren't ghosting you. **Your inbox is.**
5 signs you're losing deals before the first reply ↓

### 1
**Replies take more than 1 hour**
Interest decays fast. After an hour, you're competing with whoever answered first.

### 2
**The same question, answered 10 different ways**
- No saved answers
- No owner per channel
- Every reply starts from zero

### 3
**DMs live in 4 apps and nobody sees all of them**
Instagram, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, email. One person checks two.

### 4
**"I'll get back to you" with no follow-up date**
If it isn't scheduled, it isn't happening.

### 5
**You can't say where your last 10 customers came from**
Then you can't do more of what worked.
